## 一、 许可式添加（Permissive Connecting）的核心理念

* **利他视角**：私信/添加请求必须从“利他”角度切入，明确表达你能为对方带来的独特价值（Big Benefit）。
* **非长篇大论**：社媒私信不同于 Email，语言需精炼、精准、直击要害。
* **注重关系沉淀**：客户通过好友请求表明对其不排斥，后续需将流量从公域引导至私域（Email/微信/企业微信/Zoom等），开展深度沟通与谈判。

## 二、 线下课学员实战邀请话术（Invitation Templates）

### 1. 亮金表法（亮出大牌合作背书 + 成为 Fan）
* **话术 (1)**：
  > Hi [Name],  
  > This is Joanna from Ningbo UKnow. Supplier for QVC, HSE24, JML and Watsons for personal care products.  
  > Hope I could be one fan on your LinkedIn network. Cheers  
  > Joanna

* **话术 (2)**：
  > Hi,  
  > This is Joanna from Ningbo UKnow, I'd like to be your fan on LinkedIn.  
  > We have over 12 years of experience in supply with beauty and personal care products to DRTV industry and big brands in the world. Any questions, I am happy to help.  
  > Hope I can be your friend here. Keep up your awesome work on LinkedIn. Cheers  
  > Joanna

* **话术 (3)（眼镜行业示例）**：
  > Hi Alba,  
  > This is Sandy, Co-founder of Wenzhou Lenlook Optical. I'd like to be your fan on LinkedIn.  
  > We have over 15 years' experience in eyewear Industry, any questions, I'm happy to help. Hope I can be your friend here. Either way, keep up your awesome work on LinkedIn. Cheers,  
  > Sandy

## 四、 针对性切入策略话术（加客户前 Invitation）

### 1. 共同兴趣爱好切入
> Hi [Name],  
> I see your hobbies very interesting. I like travel, as well as photography works. I traveled to many cities in China and also to Germany, France, Spain, Czech Republic, South Africa (Brazil), India, Dubai. I wish to be your friend.  
> If you have plan to visit China, please feel free to let us know. I can share some helpful information.  
> --- Joanna Mao

### 2. 虚心请教型
> Hi [Name],  
> I like your thinking way on discussion “[Topic]”. And I learnt that you work in DRTV industry and some other famous retailer for more than 35 years, and much familiar with all the big retailers.  
> We cooperated with XXX in year XXX, I’d like to add you to my professional network on LinkedIn to learn more from you.  
> --- Joanna Mao

### 3. 开发市场/产品热销型
> Hi [Name], how are you?  
> We cooperated with one xxx buyer for our latest XXX [具体产品] with very good price and sales result. Are you interested to get more information?

### 4. 熟人/共同联系人切入（Small World 策略）
> What a small world, one of our big German buyer has very good cooperation with us, and you are his connection also. Let's connect!

### 5. 分销商开发型（强调工厂资质）
> Hi [Name],  
> Learned that you are strong in XXX items in [Country] as one professional distributor.  
> As a China supplier focus on beauty and personal care products for over 13 years, with BSCI factory audit, we sincerely wish be your friends, to expand the market together.

### 6. 互动感谢/赞美型
> Hi [Name], thanks for your comment on my post “[Title]”, give me B2B website to join and learn more. It really helps. I would like to be your friend.

### 7. 组合综合型
> Hi [Name],  
> Thanks for your care on my post. You look so nice, and you are professional in both DRTV and XXXX. I have visited in XXX, it is a nice place. I sincerely wish have chance to learn from you.

## 五、 毅冰老师经典加好友话术模板（加客户前）

1. **简洁直接型**：
   > Hi [Name], I’m Yibing from [Company], and I’ve been following your work in [industry]. Your insights on [topic] are impressive. I’d love to connect and stay updated. Best regards, Yibing
2. **行业合作型**：
   > Hi [Name], I noticed that we share a strong alignment in values and goals. I’m Yibing from [Company], working with several big names in [sector]. I believe there could be great opportunities to collaborate. Best regards, Yibing
3. **共同兴趣型**：
   > Hi [Name], I noticed you share a passion for [hobby]. I too am an avid enthusiast. I’ve traveled to [locations] and would be happy to share recommendations. Let’s connect!
4. **客户需求型**：
   > Hi [Name], I see your company is involved in [product/area]. At [Company], with over [X] years in [industry], we have successfully supported similar businesses with [benefits]. Let's connect.
5. **感谢支持型**：
   > Hi [Name], Thank you so much for your thoughtful comment on my recent post about [topic]. I really appreciate your insight and would love to connect.

## 六、 2024 年高通过率/高回复邀请模板

1. **利用专业观点**：对客户的文章/观点发表具体看法并提出创新策略。
2. **展示行业趋势**：分享行业最新趋势（如特定市场变化）并提供适配解决方案。
3. **关注公司动态**：恭喜客户公司近期获取的里程碑或新项目。
4. **分享独特数据**：提到近期整理的一手行业统计数据或趋势报告。
5. **行业资源分享**：主动提供价值极高的行业报告白皮书。
6. **帮助解决问题**：针对客户正在面临的具体业务挑战提供可行建议。
7. **提问引发兴趣**：询问客户如何保持竞争优势，引发探讨。
8. **聚焦创新解决方案**：介绍针对客户具体痛点的创新产品或服务。
9. **分享案例研究**：分享与客户同类企业的成功实操 Case Study。
10. **行业新闻触发**：结合最新行业大事件或新闻观点建立连接。

## 七、 加好友成功后的跟进与互动话术（Post-Connection Messages）

* **感谢接受**：表达荣幸，提及对方在行业内的突出贡献。
* **主动询问**：询问对方当前项目目标，探讨合作切入点。
* **分享经验**：提议分享自身在行业内积累的独特策略与洞察。
* **产品推荐**：精准匹配对方需求，推荐具有明显优势的产品。
* **探讨趋势**：就特定行业新动态交流看法。
* **提出改进建议**：针对对方业务中的微小环节提出可行的优化建议。
* **虚心请教**：就特定项目或难题向客户寻求专业建议。
* **共同项目/跨界合作**：提出双方优势互补的具体项目构想。
* **请求反馈**：邀请客户对自己正在做的行业报告或项目发表意见。
* **社交邀约**：若前往客户所在城市，邀约 Coffee Chat 进一步交流。

## 八、 许可式添加后的 10 种私信构思（最核心流量转化策略）

### 1. 感谢连接与简短介绍
* **Subject**: Great to Connect!
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for accepting my connection request! It’s great to be connected here on LinkedIn.
  > A little about myself—I'm Yibing from [Your Company], and we specialize in [products/services]. We’ve had the privilege of working with brands like [well-known brands], and I thought there might be some potential synergy between us.
  > If you’re ever looking to discuss new opportunities or explore some of our solutions, feel free to reach out anytime.
  > Best regards, Yibing

### 2. 提供帮助并分享专业见解
* **Subject**: Here to Help with Your [Industry] Needs
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for connecting! I noticed that your work in [field] aligns closely with what we do here at [Your Company]. We’ve worked on [specific projects], and I’d love to help in any way I can.
  > If you're facing any challenges or need any recommendations related to [industry/product], feel free to ask. I’d be happy to share insights or resources.
  > Best, Yibing

### 3. 分享相关行业洞察
* **Subject**: Thought You’d Find This Interesting
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > I hope you’re doing well! I came across this [article/whitepaper/study] about [relevant trend], and I thought you might find it valuable given your background.
  > If you're interested, I'd be happy to discuss some of the trends we're seeing in the industry.
  > Cheers, Yibing

### 4. 突出共同兴趣或联系
* **Subject**: Great to Connect—We Share a Lot of Similar Interests!
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for accepting my connection request! I noticed we both share an interest in [shared interest]. I’ve had the chance to work on a few projects related to this, and I’d love to hear your thoughts.
  > Looking forward to hearing from you! Best regards, Yibing

### 5. 提供解决方案应对潜在痛点
* **Subject**: A Solution for [Problem/Challenge You’ve Noticed]
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for connecting! I’ve been following your work at [Company Name], and I understand that companies in [industry] often face challenges with [pain point].
  > At [Your Company], we’ve developed [solution] that could help address this issue effectively. I’d love to discuss how we can help improve [key benefit].
  > Cheers, Yibing

### 6. 个性化介绍，侧重价值
* **Subject**: Exploring Potential Opportunities Together
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > It’s a pleasure to connect! I see that your company, [Company Name], is doing some exciting work in [industry], and I believe there may be synergy between our businesses.
  > At [Your Company], we’ve been helping businesses in [industry] improve [specific outcome], and I’d love to explore how we can add value.
  > Best regards, Yibing

### 7. 提供案例研究或成功故事
* **Subject**: How We Helped [Similar Company] Achieve [Result]
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for connecting! I wanted to quickly share a success story from one of our clients, [Client Name], in [industry]. They were facing [problem], and we helped them achieve [specific result].
  > I believe there could be similar opportunities for your business.
  > Best, Yibing

### 8. 提供免费资源或指南
* **Subject**: Free Resource for [Specific Need or Interest]
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Thanks for connecting! I wanted to share a free resource we’ve created—a [guide/ebook/whitepaper] on [topic] that provides valuable insights for your work in [industry].
  > If you'd like a copy, feel free to download it here: [Link].
  > Best regards, Yibing

### 9. 邀请参加网络研讨会或线上活动
* **Subject**: Join Me for a Webinar on [Topic]
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > Great to connect! I wanted to personally invite you to an upcoming webinar on [topic] on [Date/Time]. It’s a great opportunity to learn about [relevant insights].
  > Feel free to register here: [Link]. Hope you can join us!
  > Best regards, Yibing

### 10. 邀请安排会议讨论合作机会
* **Subject**: Let’s Schedule a Time to Connect
* **Body**:
  > Hi [Name],
  > I appreciate you accepting my connection request! I’ve been following your company’s work in [industry], and I’d love to schedule a quick 15-minute chat to explore how we might collaborate.
  > Let me know what works best for you!
  > Best regards, Yibing
