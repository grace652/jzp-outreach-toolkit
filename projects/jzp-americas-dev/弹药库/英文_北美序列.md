# 英文序列 · 美国 / 加拿大（6 封）

> **适用对象**：美国农村电力合作社（NRECA）、市政公用事业（APPA）、EPC 与电气分销商（NAED）、数据中心开发商；加拿大省级公用事业、EPC、EFC 体系分销商。
> **主线诉求**：交期。**副线**：UL / cUL / CSA 三证齐备。
> **骨架来源**：`foreign-trade-email-dev` skill · `references/03-mail-group序列.md`（Mail Group 打法）+ `references/02-开发信.md`。
> **规范**：首封 ≤150 词、跟进 ≤120 词、Subject 3–6 词、无 spam 触发词、半角标点。
>
> ⚠️ **所有 `[方括号]` 必须替换为真实信息。** 我方向未提供的（如交期承诺、产能、出口年限）一律留 `（待填）`，**不要编造**。
> ⚠️ **客户名与项目细节的引用需先取得书面授权**，见作战手册 7.4 节。未授权的用脱敏表述。

## 第 1 封 · D0 · 交期 + 认证

### Subject 备选
1. `Pad-mount transformers - [X]-week delivery`
2. `UL & CSA pad-mount supply`
3. `Distribution transformer lead time`

### 正文

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your Name] at JZP Power Transformer, a Chinese manufacturer of
liquid-immersed distribution transformers.

Most of our customers in North America are dealing with the same problem
right now: quoted lead times of 26 to 40 weeks for standard distribution
units, and longer for three-phase pad-mount.

We can deliver in [X] weeks.

Our product lines relevant to you:
- Single-phase pad-mounted, 25-167 kVA
- Three-phase pad-mounted, up to 5,000 kVA
- Substation and dry-type transformers

We hold UL (E541626, E541627), cUL and CSA C227.4 certification, and
manufacture to IEEE C57.12.00/.28/.34/.90.

Would a delivery schedule for a specific unit be useful? Send me the
kVA rating and voltage class and I will come back with a firm date.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Title] | JZP Power Transformer Inc.
[Email] | [Phone]
```

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 第 1 封（问题共鸣 + 单一钩子）。毅冰「价值输出而非疑问」原则——先给方案再问需求。
- **为什么这么写**：北美当前最痛的是交期，不是价格。开头直接用客户正在经历的问题建立相关性，第三段才亮出我方能力。**主动说出「26–40 周」这个行业现状，证明你懂他的处境。**
- **认证放在产品线之后**：先讲「我能给你什么」，再讲「我凭什么」。顺序反了会像资质罗列。
- **待确认**：
  - `[X] weeks` —— **必须是真实产能承诺**，不可夸大。这是全序列的核心承诺，一次失约就毁掉定位。
  - 若目标客户是数据中心开发商，可在产品线后加一句 `Data center and commercial projects are not subject to Build America, Buy America requirements, so we remain fully eligible for those.`（**仅在确认对方项目属性后使用**）
- **合规提示**：不回避「中国制造商」身份。当前环境下含糊反而引发怀疑，主动说明边界（我们不做 ≥69kV 变电站设备）更能建立信任。

## 第 2 封 · D3 · 产品规格

### Subject 备选
1. `Pad-mount spec sheet`
2. `25-167 kVA pad-mount data`
3. `Specification sheet attached`

### 正文

```
Dear [Name],

Following up on my note about lead times.

Attached is our specification sheet for single-phase pad-mounted
transformers, 25 to 167 kVA, loop feed, ANSI/IEEE design.

Key points:
- Rated 25-167 kVA, HV up to [voltage], LV 120/240 V
- Loop or radial feed, dead-front or live-front
- Mineral oil or [alternative fluid] (待填)
- Tested to IEEE C57.12.90

For three-phase units up to 5,000 kVA I can send a separate sheet - just
tell me the kVA and voltage class you work with most.

Is there a current project where a quoted delivery date would help?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 第 2 封（价值物 + 收窄提问）。
- **为什么这么写**：第一封没回应，第二封不要追问「看了吗」，而是**再给一样有用的东西**。附件是规格单，不是公司介绍册。
- **待确认**：`HV up to [voltage]`、油种、附件文件。规格单请用真实 PDF，**不要用 ZIP 或 RAR**（skill 硬规则）。
- **注意**：附件 ≤3M，单封 ≤5 个。

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 第 3 封（社会证明）。

## 第 4 封 · D12 · 关税与落地成本透明化

### Subject 备选
1. `Landed cost breakdown`
2. `Duty and landed cost`
3. `Transparent pricing`

### 正文

```
Dear [Name],

One thing that causes problems later: duty.

Chinese-origin distribution transformers currently carry a combined duty
load of roughly [XX]% into the United States (Section 301 plus Section
232). Any FOB-only quote you receive will look cheaper than it really is.

So we quote two ways:
1. FOB [Port], with a duty estimate at the current rate
2. DDP [Destination], our responsibility to your door

We also note the tariff schedule date on every quote, because these
rates have changed twice in 2026 alone.

If you would rather run the numbers with your own customs broker, we
will supply the full HTS classification so you can verify.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 第 4 封（主动暴露不利信息，换取信任）。
- **为什么这么写**：毅冰方法论里最重要的一条——**主动讲清楚对你不利的事，客户才会相信你讲的其他事**。在约 50% 税负下，只报 FOB 是最短视的做法，会在清关时炸掉关系。
- **待确认**：
  - `[XX]%` 必须用**报关行的书面税则意见**填入，不要用手册第 2 章的估算值。
  - `[Port]`、`[Destination]` 填实际港口与目的地。
- **合规提示**：手册第 2 章表格为估算，**未取得书面税则意见前不要向客户报出承诺性数字**。

## 第 5 封 · D20 · 产能预留（blanket PO）

### Subject 备选
1. `Capacity reservation for 2027`
2. `Blanket order option`
3. `Reserving production slots`

### 正文

```
Dear [Name],

A practical suggestion.

Lead times across the industry are expected to stay tight through 2027 -
most of the new North American capacity does not come online until
2027-2028.

Rather than quoting one unit at a time, we can hold a production slot for
you against a blanket order covering your expected 2027 requirement. You
release against it as projects firm up; we keep the slot reserved.

This is how several of our customers handle their annual pad-mount
program.

If you can share a rough annual volume and the kVA mix, I will put
together a reservation proposal with pricing tiers.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 第 5 封（把一次性交易转为框架性关系）。
- **为什么这么写**：在交期紧张的市场里，「产能预留」比降价更有吸引力——它直接回应了客户最大的不确定性。**这一封是整个序列里转化率最高的一封。**
- **待确认**：产能预留的具体条款（最小承诺量、价格梯度、违约处理）需内部先定，**不要在没有内部方案时发出这一封**。
- **注意**：`several of our customers` 若无法证实，改为 `we can offer`。

## 第 6 封 · D30 · 展会 / 样品

### Subject 备选
1. `Meeting at DISTRIBUTECH`
2. `Sample unit proposal`
3. `Test unit for your evaluation`

### 正文

```
Dear [Name],

Last note from me on this thread.

Two options if you would like to move forward:

1. We will be at [Exhibition Name] in [City], [Date]. I can hold 30
   minutes for your team.
2. We can supply a test unit for your own evaluation and testing - a
   25 kVA or 50 kVA single-phase pad-mount is the usual starting point.

Either way, if the timing is not right, tell me when to come back and I
will note it. No need to reply otherwise.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 中文批注
- **骨架**：Mail Group 收尾封（给台阶 + 明确退路）。
- **为什么这么写**：毅冰方法论中「给客户台阶」的典型应用。最后一句 `No need to reply otherwise` 大幅降低回复成本——**反而会提高回复率**。
- **展会建议**：优先 **NRECA PowerXchange & TechAdvantage 2027（2027-02-12~17，圣迭戈）** 与 **DISTRIBUTECH 2027（2027-03-01~04，亚特兰大）**。
- **待确认**：`[Exhibition Name]` `[City]` `[Date]` 与展位号。

## 序列使用说明

| 项 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| **触发节奏** | D0 / D3 / D7 / D12 / D20 / D30 |
| **回应后** | 立即停止序列，转入询盘回复流程（`foreign-trade-email-dev` 的 `references/05-询盘报价与议价.md`） |
