# 外延 · 下单后执行

> **默认不加载。** 仅当用户明确提到「催款邮件」「出货通知」「单据确认」「PI」「延期交货」时读取。
> ⚠️ 用户已明确表示**本 skill 核心不含下单后交流**，这是外延。

---

## 一、边界说明

下单后 ≠ 开发阶段。这里的邮件**目标是执行**，不是推进成交。语气、节奏、KPI 都不同。

---

## 二、PI 与收款

### 做 PI 后确认账号
```
Well received your PO for [Product]. Attached please find our Proforma Invoice
(PI) for [Amount], kindly stamp and sign back ASAP.
```

### 催款三档（从轻到重）

**第 1 档 · 直接问**
```
Dear [Name],
Have you already settled the rest payment? Please help to send me the bank slip
for record. Thanks.
```

**第 2 档 · 附单据顺势提**
```
Dear [Name],
We're really pleased to inform you that everything is OK. Attached is the copy
of B/L for your review.
Could you please balance the payment for us please? And we'll send you the
original B/L with the documents accordingly.
```

**第 3 档 · 最后通牒（慎用）**
```
Dear [Name],
We are unable to understand why we have received no reply and no payment yet
till now.
We feel that we have shown reasonable patience and treated you along overdue
period. We cannot afford any delay at the moment.
Please reply us immediately for balancing the payment. If not we have to take
action in law and report this claim to the insurance company.
```

**铁律**：**没确认款项实际到账前，决不凭水单提早安排电放或寄送正本提单。**

### 小额款项
优先 **PayPal / Western Union**，避免电汇手续费扣减导致实到金额不足。

---

## 三、出货与单据

| 环节 | 要点 |
|---|---|
| 告知船期 | 给全 船名 / 航次 / ETD / ETA |
| 告知已发货 | 附加分动作（如现场装柜照片） |
| 和客户确认单据 | 逐项列清单据名称与份数 |
| 单据派送 | 给快递单号 |
| 单据送达确认 | 主动确认，避免客户说「没收到」 |
| 货到港提醒 | 提前提醒，避免滞港费 |

**监装拍照**：集装箱正面 / 空箱 / 50% 装载 / 满箱 / 封条 —— 五张。

## 四、延期交货

**按客户熟悉度分三版**：

**详细版（重要客户）**
```
Dear [Name],
Sorry to inform you that we have to postpone the shipment 2-3 weeks later.
According to our internal inspection we found the surface treating was not
perfect. So I asked my team to arrange the re-work immediately, to make
everything better.
I take it for granted that quality is the top priority for both of us for
running orders. And I really want you to be proud of our items without standing
performance. So please help to give us more time.
Thanks & best regards,
[Name]
```

**老客户版（可极简）**
```
Hi [Name],
The order [Number] has to be delayed to [Date], due to the labor shortage.
Please approve it today.
[Name]
```

**新客户版（要客气）**
```
Dear [Name],
We are really sorry to inform you that the ETD for PO# [Number] will be delayed
to [Date], because we purchased the package material late.
We will pay close attention to this order and will not postpone the shipment
date again. Please help to approve the updated ETD. Thank you in advance!
```

**写作要点**（毅冰在《45个经典话术》里明确点过）：
1. **不要解释太多** —— 长篇大论会让客户生气，怪你没用
2. **不要推卸责任** —— 写别人做错了，但和客户交流的是你
3. **注意目的** —— 你的目的是让客户确认新时间，`please approve` 一定要写上
4. **短**
