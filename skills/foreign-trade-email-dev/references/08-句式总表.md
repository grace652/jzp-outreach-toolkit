# 08 · 可复用英文句式总表

> 按环节归类。`[ ]` 为待填参数。全部取自毅冰素材原文，句式结构未改。
> 只收**开发 → 成交前**环节的句式；下单后/售后句式见 `out-of-scope/下单后执行.md`。

---

## 一、开场（证明不是群发）

```
Glad to find your e-mail address from website.
Glad to get your contact info from [Fair] / my friend [Name].
Came across [Company] while looking into [buyer's market].
This is [Name] from [Company]. Glad to get your info from my [Country] friend [Referrer].
```

## 二、自我介绍与专业定位

```
Glad to hear that you're in the market for [Product]. We specialize in this field for several years, with the strength of high quality and competitive price.
We supply [Product] to [Market] for several years.
We're [Company], [years] manufacturer of [Product] for [target buyers] in [Country].
We had the honor to serve [Big Name] in [Country].
```

## 三、信任背书

```
We have already passed the factory audit from [Big Buyer], SGS & BSCI.
All our items are coming with certification.
We are ready for signing contract with you in case you need it.
With us your money will be in safe and your business will be in safe.
```

## 四、钩子与 CTA

```
FREE SAMPLES could be sent for your evaluation!
Should you have any question, please don't hesitate to contact me.
Worth a quick look? Just reply "[Keyword]" and I'll arrange it.
Please help to check and advise your comments.
```

## 六、回复询盘

```
Thanks a lot for your inquiry!
Regarding the [Product], I have to check the price again and send you the offer sheet in [X] days.
Well, received. I will check with our engineer (/boss/director/manager/colleague) and reply you soonest!
Concerning your inquired item [Product], please find our detailed offer sheet with MOQ [Amount] in attachment.
```

## 七、报价

```
Below / Attachment is new items and some information about our bestseller, our factory and certificate.
If any demand, I will provide the offer sheet with samples immediately.
To be candid with you, it is the basic price, and will be fluctuated due to the different quantity.
Should you have any questions, this is welcome.
```

## 八、价格谈判与降本

```
Price is important, but quality counts for much more.
We could reduce the unit price if we change the packaging of [Product] into poly bag.
Sure, our models with top quality! If the price is not suitable in your price range, could you pls accept to do a little change? The price will be reduced [X]%.
I'm sorry we cannot accept your target price USD [Amount] with [Packaging]. That means USD [Amount] is our floor price. Please help to understand our situation and back us.
```

## 九、涨价

```
As I mentioned in the previous email, the price was based on [condition] only.
Please help to understand that the exchange rate / raw material has changed a lot recently.
Attached the price breakdown for your review.
We could make a concession for [X]% only, and we'll try our best to keep the quality as before.
```

## 十、跟进（未看 / 已读未回）

```
Could I remind you to check my email dated [Date]?
It's been a time no any news hear from you. How are you recently?
I have sent you a quotation dated [Date], have you got it?
May I know what's your idea about it?
I think you must too busy to reply me or do you still have something into consideration.
It so, could you frankly let me know any of your worries? Such as price, quality, payment terms, demand or others?
```

```

I could push my factory for sampling as quickly as possible and let you receive in [X] days.

# 热卖切入
Here attached the photos for your top rated items for the [Country] market. Just for your reference.

# 品质切入

# 情怀切入

# 「无赖」切入

```

## 十四、推进成交 / 试单

```
We haven't heard from you since we sent the offer sheet. I wonder whether you need any further information before placing an order.
In order to sweeten the pot, I'm okay to accept the trial order with low MOQ.
Could you share which part is still open? We'll work out a concrete proposal for that specific point.
```

## 十五、竞单挽回

```
I understand you have placed the order elsewhere. No hard feelings at all.
Just one thing I'd like to know for my own improvement: was it price, lead time, or something else?
Should you need a backup vendor for [Product] in the future, I'll be glad to be one of your options.
```

## 十六、长期培育

```
Just want to share with you our new arrivals for [Season]. Attached the e-catalogue for your reference.
A few items we believe fit the [Country] market very well.
I came across an industry report about [Market/Category] recently. A few points you might find useful.
It's been [X] months since the last shipment. How is [Product] selling?
```

## 十七、休假与自动回复

```
I'm not in the office from [Date] till [Date]. Your e-mails will not be answered during this period.
I'm currently on sick leave / annual leave. My emails are not handled regularly.
For any trade issues in the 2nd period kindly reach my colleagues as follows and cc me the emails.
I will be on Annual Leave from [Date] to [Date]. My colleague [Name] from [Company] will handle my work during this period.
```

## 十八、行业信息融入

> 完整方法（决策流程 / 筛选标准 / 避坑 / 回退）见 `09-行业信息融入.md`。

**A 型 · 行业信息开场**
```
A new DOE efficiency rule for distribution transformers came out this June.
Several utilities are re-checking their specs because of it.
Our [Product] is already built to it — want the spec sheet?
```

**B 型 · 客户自身事件开场**（congrats 只用一次，紧跟实质内容）
```
Saw [Company] won the [Project] contract last month.
If the transformer package is still open, we could quote against the spec.
```

**C 型 · 把话题拉回客户业务**（毅冰《万能模板》§1.10 原句式）
```
As know from your website, you are a distributor of [Brand], right?
```

**D 型 · 同类产品讨论**（《十天函电》法，一句就够）
```
As I know, lots of [Country] [buyer type] begin to use [trend].
```

**回退用的通用价值句**（无行业数据时）
```
No industry data handy this time — one thing that has not changed:
lead times on [product] are still the bottleneck for most [buyer type].
```
> ⚠️ 回退句**不得带具体数字**，也不要硬凑成行业科普。

---

## 用法提醒

1. **句式是骨架不是成品**：必须替换 `[ ]` 里的参数，并接上具体情境
2. **同一封邮件不要堆 3 个以上句式**，会显得模板化
3. **英文标点后记得加空格**，不要用中文标点
4. **优先用 `Could you please help to...` 而不是 `Please...`**，商务场合更稳妥
