# 04 · 跟进邮件

> 来源：《万能开发信模板100篇》4.2–4.3；《39篇外贸邮件模版》「报价后客户跟进系列」；《展会75封展会跟进模板》；《外贸高手客户成交技巧2》案例3-6

---

## 一、先分清 Push 和 Follow-up

**这是跟进的核心纪律**（《成交技巧2》案例3-6）：

| | Push（催促） | Follow-up（跟进） |
|---|---|---|
| 出发点 | 我要订单 | 帮客户推进决策 |
| 客户感受 | 被掐脖子 | 被支持 |
| 频率 | 连续催，催到客户用「消失」应对 | 有节奏，每次带新价值 |
| 结果 | 客户把业务员拉进黑名单 | 客户愿意回 |

**反面案例**（别这么做）：
- 案例1-4「Overwhelming push（让人崩溃的催促）」
- 案例1-3「Delay, delay and delay（难以容忍的拖延）」
- 案例1-7「Lie a lie and a lie（一个接一个的谎言）」

## 二、场景 A：客户没看邮件

判据：发了回盘/报价/样品/PI 一个多星期，**没有阅读回执，也没查看记录**。

### 工具
`Readnotify`（readnotify.com）—— 注意有时效性。

### 模板 1（简单提醒）
```
Hi [Name],
Could I remind you to check my email dated [Date]?
Waiting for your reply soon.
```

### 模板 2（带一次内容摘要，降低客户点开成本）
```
Hi [Name],
It's been a time no any news hear from you. How are you recently?
I have sent you a quotation dated [Date], have you got it?
If so, what's your idea about it?
If not, could you take few minutes to review it as follows?
Your earlier reply will be highly appreciated.
```

### 模板 3（电话说好会回、3–4 天后仍无消息 —— 迂回催促）
```
Hi [Name],
I'm sorry to disturb you again.
However, disturb is not my purpose. As you know every company has his or her
rules for every sales, and we should feedback the valuable customer's feedback
on weekly meeting. So that we could help them better.
My friend, dear [Name], any of your idea about our [Product] now? Or you have
any other worries, hope you can be frankly let us know?
Waiting for your reply soon.
```

## 三、场景 B：客户看了邮件但没回

### 先诊断（9 条常见原因）
A 出差、假期　B 对产品不感兴趣　C 邮件没有亮点　**D 不想更换供应商**　E 报价太高　F 付款方式不是想要的　G 交期问题　H 需求不明确　I 其他（个人或亲人病重等）

### 四种跟进话术

**第一种 · 直接询问式**
```
Hi [Name],
Tks for reading my email just now.
May I know what's your idea about it?
Your earlier reply will be highly appreciated.
```

**第二种 · 从商业角度，让客户坦白顾虑**
```
Hi [Name],
Tks for reading my email. I think you must too busy to reply me or do you
still have something into consideration.
It so, could you frankly let me know any of your worries?
Such as price, quality, payment terms, demand or others?
So that we could know how to help & support you.
We are very sincerely to cooperation, no matter your answer is positive or
negative. We highly appreciate it, just hope you can open your heart.
Waiting for your reply soon.
```

**第三种 · 从朋友角度**
```
Hi [Name],
Long time no hear from you. I appreciate for your kindly to check my email again.
I think you should be not convenient to reply my email at moment or do you still
have some worries about our products? Such as price, quality, payment, demand or
others.
If so, hope you can be frankly informed me so that we could know how to help &
support you.
Dear [Name], I really appreciate for the god to let me know you. Business may
past, but we still a good friend, yeah? And I think you will also not refuse a
friend to help you by her sincerely heart, right?
Your earlier reply will be highly appreciated.
Sincerely,
[Name]
```

**第四种 · 直接罗列解决方案（最有效，降低客户开口成本）**
```
Dear [Name],
Tks a lot for kindly to check my email. I think you must too busy to reply me
or do you still have something into consideration?

1. If the MOQ problem
   - We can support you [Qty] as the first cooperation to try the market.
2. If the shipping cost of the sample
   A. You can just transfer us some deposit, we'll return it to you when you
      confirm the order.
   B. In order to show our sincerity, we can bear half of it and you afford
      the balance, how's your idea?
3. If the price
   You can test our sample at first, after getting the good feedback, shall we
   discuss price again basic on its real values?
4. If the payment terms
   We can support you 50% T/T in advance, balance against the B/L at sight.
   Also L/C will be also acceptable.

Or do you still have any other worries, pls be frankly let me know so that we
could know how to help you.
```

## 四、场景 C：报价后客户跟进（5 封序列）

**每封一个切入角度，依次推进。**

| # | 切入 | 核心 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **样品切入** | 主动提出快速打样，给一个具体天数 |
| 2 | **热卖产品切入** | 发对方市场 top-rated 款的图片 |
| 3 | **产品品质切入** | 讲质控投入，提出可发技术文件 |
| 4 | **情怀切入** | 站在客户角度理解他要权衡的（质量/定价/利润/交期/促销计划），提出可展示「为其他大客户做过什么」 |
| 5 | **「无赖」切入** | 自嘲 + 约具体通话时间，把主动权拿回来 |
| 6 | **行业信息切入** | 带一条与客户业务相关的外部行业信息（标准 / 市场 / 客户动态）。取数、筛选与写法见 `09-行业信息融入.md`；**取不到就退回上面 5 个角度，不要编造** |

**第 1 封 · 样品切入**
```
Hi [Name],
How are you doing? Have you reviewed my offer sheet which simply quoted a
[tour booth of Canton Fair]?
I could push my factory for sampling as quickly as possible and let you
receive in [X] days. And we could discuss further issues accordingly.
Thank you.
Best regards,
[Name]
```

**第 2 封 · 热卖产品切入**
```
Dear [Name],
Here attached the photos for your top rated items for the [Country] market.
Just for your reference.
Please keep me informed for any questions.
Best regards,
[Name]
```

**第 3 封 · 产品品质切入**
```
Dear [Name],
Thanks for your inquiry from the [Fair].
We always do our best to keep prices as low as possible without sacrificing
quality and we are constantly investigating new methods of quality improvement.
If you have interest we are willing to send you some technical files for your
reference.
We really need your comments to go ahead.
Best regards,
[Name]
```

**第 4 封 · 情怀切入**
```
Dear [Name],
As we haven't heard from you since we sent you our offer sheet I wonder whether
you require any further information before placing an order.
We absolutely understand you will consider a majority of issues such as quality,
pricing margin, lead time or promotion planning. According to our full experience
in this field we could show you something which we did for our other heavy
customers.
I'm sure our further discussion could break the ice and satisfy both of us.
Best regards,
[Name]
```

**第 5 封 · 「无赖」切入**
```
Dear [Name],
Still no comments from your side. I think you are really busy after the tough
business trip to [Fair].
So I attach the offer sheet again. Please help to review it at your convenience.
And I plan to call you next Monday around 3:00 p.m. at your time.
Please advise me if not workable then.
Kind regards,
[Name]
```

**第 6 封 · 行业信息切入**（取数、筛选与写法见 `09-行业信息融入.md`）
```
Dear [Name],
A new DOE efficiency rule for distribution transformers came out this June.
Several utilities are re-checking their specs because of it.
Our [Product] is already built to it — want the spec sheet?
Best regards,
[Name]
```
> 上述为**骨架示例**：信息本身与 `[Product]` 须按客户实际市场替换。
> **取不到与客户匹配的信息时，不要用这一封** —— 退回前 5 个角度，不要编造。

## 五、场景 D：展会来源，按客户分层跟进

**展后第一件事不是群发，是先分层。** 六类客户，六套跟进策略：

| 客户类型 | 定义 | 跟进要点 |
|---|---|---|
| **成单客户** | 展会当场签约或付定金 | **第一个 contact**，目的是顺利执行订单 |
| **要样客户** | 展会上要了样品 | 快速寄样 + 主动承担运费以显诚意 |
| **约访客户** | 约了后续会面 | 确认时间地点，安排接待 |
| **坐谈客户** | 展位深谈过 | 直接给报价 + 他在展会上看过的产品 |
| **名片客户** | 只交换名片、无其他信息 | **先做背景调查**（做什么产品/规模/采购需求/行业年限），再写跟进信；信息不足也要先发一封抓住展会印象 |
| **未到访/擦肩** | 走过展位没停 | 用「展会现场火热」的照片 + 热门产品切入 |

**展后当晚快速跟进（两类）**

```
客人挑了样品要报价
Hi [Name],
Glad to meet you in our booth today!
Attached is the list of your selected items with photos. Please help to check
if everything is OK.
I will check the prices one by one with my colleagues after the fair. And you
could receive the offer sheet with details next Monday.
Please don't hesitate to contact me for any other questions. Thank you!
Kind regards,
[Name]
```

```
客人带了自己的样品来
Hello [Name],
Thanks for visiting our booth with a short chat!
Will you stay in [City] in the coming days?
My boss will come here the day after tomorrow. If you have time we could have a
meeting then to discuss your project. Or we can pay a visit to your hotel lobby
and show you our similar samples.
Is it workable please?
Kind regards,
[Name]
```

**「让客户想起你是谁」**
```
Dear [Name],
This is [MyName] from [MyCompany].
Our company has just participated in [Fair] recently. Our company came out very
well, and our products attracted rare reviews by global buyers. (附展位火热图片)
Here are some of our company's best-selling products, you can see if you are
interested. If yes, please feel free to contact me at any time.
Looking forward to your reply.
```

## 六、节奏建议

| 时点 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 触达后 +3 天 | 第 1 封跟进（未看/已读未回） |
| +7 天 | 换角度切入（报价后第 1 封） |
| +14 天 | 换角度（第 2–3 封） |
| +21 天 | 情怀切入 + 约电话 |
| +30 天 | 「无赖」切入，明确约通话时间 |
| 任意时点 | **行业信息切入**（有匹配信息时优先用，见 `09-行业信息融入.md`）|

**每次必须换主题、换角度、带新信息。** 重复同一封 = 催促。

> 「新信息」的**外部来源**（行业动态 / 标准法规 / 客户业务新闻）见 `09-行业信息融入.md`；
> 取不到就沿用自家素材（热卖产品 / 品质 / 案例）。**不要编造。**

## 七、自检

- [ ] 这次跟进带了**新信息**吗（不是重复上一封）
- [ ] Subject 换了吗
- [ ] 是 Follow-up（帮客户推进）还是 Push（我要订单）
- [ ] 给了客户**低成本的回复路径**（比如「回一个词就行」）
- [ ] 报价后序列用了不同的切入角度，不是同一角度重复 5 次
- [ ] 若用了行业信息切入：只用了**一个**触发点、可追溯出处、未引用竞品、未含交期价格数字（见 `09-行业信息融入.md` §九）
